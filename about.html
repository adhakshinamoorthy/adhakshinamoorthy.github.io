What I do

I design and build cloud systems on .NET and Azure. I have spent more than 18 years building software, and the last 4.5+ years shaping technical architecture: how systems are split, secured, deployed, and kept healthy in production.

Recently I led a multitenant SaaS ERP platform built on .NET, React, and Azure. The work covered tenant isolation, a clean modular backend, secure handling of secrets and files, and turning an inconsistent codebase into a structured architecture the team could keep growing.

Why I built .NET Atlas

I learn best by building, then writing down what worked and what did not. .NET Atlas is that notebook, made public: clear guides, honest trade-offs, and runnable samples, so other developers can move faster than I did.
